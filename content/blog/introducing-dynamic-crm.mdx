Most CRMs were designed for sales teams. They talk about **leads**, **accounts** and **opportunities**, and
everyone else has to translate. A clinic doesn't have "contacts"; it has **patients**. A real estate office doesn't
close "deals"; it closes **property sales**.

## Why "dynamic"?

In Dynamic-CRM, every workspace decides:

- **What things are called.** Rename Contacts, Companies, Deals, Activities and Tasks to whatever your team says out loud.
- **What information you track.** Add custom fields such as dates, dropdowns, currencies and checkboxes to any record.
- **How work flows.** Define your own pipeline stages and drag records between them.

## Start from a template

Pick *Clinic*, *Real Estate*, *Agency* or *General business* when you sign up. We pre-configure the terminology, the
most common fields and a sensible pipeline. You can change all of it later.
