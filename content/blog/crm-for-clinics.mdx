The Clinic template turns Dynamic-CRM into a lightweight practice tool:

| Generic CRM | In your clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Contact | Patient |
| Company | Insurance provider |
| Deal | Appointment |
| Activity | Visit note |
| Task | Follow-up |

## The appointment pipeline

Appointments flow through **Booked → Confirmed → Checked in → Completed**, with a **No-show** column for the ones
that don't happen. Drag cards as patients arrive and your front desk always knows the state of the day.

## Fields worth adding

The template includes date of birth, blood type and insurance number. Practices often add:

1. Preferred language
2. Referring doctor
3. Consent on file (yes/no)

Go to **Settings → Fields** in your workspace to add them. No developer is needed.

> Dynamic-CRM is a general-purpose CRM, not a certified medical records system. Store clinical data according to your
> local regulations.
