Custom fields let each workspace capture exactly what it needs. A few rules of thumb:

## Pick the narrowest type

- Use **Dropdown** when the answer comes from a known list. It keeps reports clean.
- Use **Date** rather than text for anything you might sort by.
- Use **Currency** for money. It formats consistently everywhere.

## Show only what matters in lists

Every field has a *Show in list* toggle. Long notes and rarely-used details belong on the record page, not in the table.

## Archive, don't delete

Archiving a field hides it from forms and tables but **keeps the data** on existing records. If you change your mind,
restore it and everything is still there.
